Before you message me

Who is this for?

Creators, brands and startups that need short-form, motion or flow edits and don't have an editor in-house.

How do we get started?

Email me or send a DM on Instagram. Tell me what you're making and when it's due, and I'll reply with the scope and a quote.

What do I need to send?

Raw footage, brand assets, reference videos and any notes. No script is fine.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length and how much motion the edit needs. We agree on dates before I start, so you always know what's next.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Revision rounds are agreed up front, so changes stay quick and predictable.

Can we work together regularly?

Yes. Many clients come back for a steady flow of edits. We can set up a monthly arrangement that fits your pace.
